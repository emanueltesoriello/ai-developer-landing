FAQ

The questions everyone asks.

Who owns the code?

You do. Full IP transfer, your repositories, your infrastructure.

What if your senior engineer changes or is unavailable?

Another senior takes over with zero restart: the dedicated machine, the indexed codebase, the conventions and the full project history stay in place. Continuity is a contractual guarantee, not a promise.

Who will my senior engineer be?

We match them to your stack and project, which is why we start from a short brief. You meet them on the scoping call, before any commitment.

Is our data safe?

Each client gets a physically dedicated, isolated machine: your VPN, your credentials, under NDA. Nothing is shared across clients.

What if the AI can't do something?

The senior engineer does it. The guarantee is the output at senior level. How much is AI and how much is human is our problem, not yours.

How is this different from Devin or Copilot?

Those are tools: you pilot them, you review them, you own the risk. Here a senior pilots the AI and signs off on the code. You buy a result, not a licence.

Can we scale up or down, or stop?

Yes, monthly, with 30 days' notice. Move between tiers as the project needs, or grow into a full team with design and PM. Full terms in the service agreement.
